Welcome to Universe

Universe was created as a private space for those who shared a history that began in 2020. 
What started as a small group of people slowly formed into UnderCover Mafia in 2021 - a quiet but determined crew built on trust, loyalty 
and presence rather than attention. It was never about numbers or visibility, but about connection. 

By 2024, life began to take priority, and moving forward as a crew was no longer possible. 
Instead of letting everything disappear, Universe was created as a way to preserve what had been built. 

It became a private server where the story, the memories, and the bond could continue without pressure or expectations.
Over time, the idea of keeping everything closed no longer felt right. The foundation of Universe became the starting point for something new 
A public space shaped by the same values, now known as Pantheverse.
